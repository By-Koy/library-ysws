The pitch:

Learning and teaching the next in line

A project can be anything technical, but it all starts with the guide. You may create your own or use someone else's, just make sure to make it yours!

Making projects

Create your dreams!

Now that you've found a guide,

it's time to make it come alive

Make a project following the guide's steps and explanations.

You will also have to document the process with devlogs

You will be graded on both understanding and creativity,

so go nuts!

After your project gets rated, you will gain pages depending on the time you spent on it.

Pages can be used in the tech shop to get grants for anything that can help you make the next project.

Writing your book

An author of dreams

Some projects won't have guides,

this is when you make your own!

A guide is a document including steps to make a project and an explanation of its principles

Your guide will be graded on its storytelling, how well it explains its principles, and of course, creativity!

After your guide gets rated, you will gain ink for the time you spent researching and every project using it.

*When making a guide you will also gain pages for the project you made

Ink is then used in the hobby shop to get you all sorts of fun, creative rewards!

It all culminates in the main event:

A hackathon with a visit to the biggest library in the world

Contestestants will go to the British (or similarly big) Library to learn as much information they would like to incorporate into their projects, while meeting and getting to know everyone :3

After they've learned cool stuff everyone will go to the venue where they will pick their teams, and then spend the next 5 (hopefully >.<) days creating and mastering their projects
